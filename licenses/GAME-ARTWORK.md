## Verification and provenance

- The 10 App Store entries use Apple’s official iTunes search/lookup metadata for the Chinese store. Track title, bundle identifier and seller were checked to avoid companion apps or imitations. Each icon is the native 1024 × 1024 rendition provided by Apple’s image CDN, not an enlarged 512-pixel download.
- Chinese Clash of Clans and Clash Royale store listings credit Tencent Tianyou as the regional seller. The games and their underlying brands are Supercell properties. Plants vs. Zombies is a PopCap/EA property; the App Store listing for PvZ2 credits PopCap.
- Steam poster URLs belong to the official Steam store image CDN. NARAKA: BLADEPOINT is app 1203220 (24 Entertainment / NetEase Games); Wallpaper Engine is app 431960 (Wallpaper Engine Team). Both `_2x` poster files natively decode as 600 × 900.
- The Fusion image comes directly from the original Bilibili video by 蓝飘飘fly, UID 3546619314178489, titled 植物大战僵尸融合版1.0预览版宣传片. The poster is 1920 × 1080. It is an author-made fan-game promotional image using the Plants vs. Zombies property; credit applies to the Fusion creators and underlying rights holders. It was not sourced from unofficial mobile clones, mirrors or similarly named accounts.
- 造梦西游4 uses the official 4399 publisher article’s screenshot of the original 洪荒大劫篇 web game. Its native resolution is only 500 × 320; this limitation is recorded rather than presenting an enlarged image as high resolution. The source article links the original web game at https://www.4399.com/flash/151038.htm .
- 造梦西游5 uses 4399’s officially published 1920 × 1080 Spring Festival wallpaper for 上古天帝篇, preserving the original title logo and watermark. This is the web game, not a mobile adaptation.

## Integrity

SHA-256 values are recorded in the workspace manifest `work/games-artwork-manifest.json`. All 15 files were checked to be decodable images with the native dimensions listed above. The manifest is a build-time sourcing record outside the published project.
